Explanations for the pandoc comparison (run_benchmark.py, pandoc 3.1.3, 20 runs per timing).

Agreement after the author fix: 16 LaTeX fixtures, all metrics compared; 2 disagreements remain.

1. figures.tex: figures_top_level ours=4 pandoc=5; figures_with_caption ours=6 pandoc=5.
   The starred figure environment figure* (caption* with no number) is dropped by pandoc:
   pandoc returns no Figure for it. Our reader keeps it as a figure, which is what LaTeX
   does (figure* is a float). Ours is right; pandoc's count is 5 = 3 top-level figures
   plus 2 subfigures. The two counted differences come from that one environment.

BibTeX (5 files). Metrics compared only for files pandoc reads.
2. basic.bib: pandoc fails with "unexpected '('" on @Inproceedings(...). BibTeX allows
   parentheses as entry delimiters, so pandoc is wrong here; ours reads all 5 entries.
3. malformed.bib: pandoc fails at line 3 on a missing comma after the key. Our reader
   warns and skips that entry, which is the documented recovery. Pandoc gives no output,
   so this file gives no structure comparison.
4. crossref-biblatex.bib: pandoc was killed for out-of-memory under a 4 GB ceiling
   (exit 251, "out of memory"). A minimal file reproduces it: two entries whose crossref
   fields name each other (a cycle). Pandoc's reader does not stop on the cycle; ours
   follows crossref up to 8 levels and stops, with resolved values. BibTeX itself does
   not follow a cycle. Ours is the correct behaviour; pandoc is not robust here.
5. strings-accents.bib: after two harness corrections (typographic apostrophe and the
   https://doi.org prefix are the same data), no disagreements remain. Pandoc keeps
   O’Brien and the DOI URL form; we keep the input apostrophe and return the bare DOI.
   Both are valid representations.
6. Earlier disagreements that were ours and wrong, now fixed (listed for transparency):
   - article-basic: three authors instead of two. A line break inside \author is part of an
     author's block (the affiliation), and only \and separates authors. Fixed in
     src/latex/parser.rs; golden output updated.
   - Links: pandoc turns resolved \ref into links. The comparison now counts our resolved
     references with explicit links.
   - crossref year: the year is inherited from the parent entry (BibTeX crossref). The
     harness now uses the resolved value.

Where pandoc does more (not measured here, stated for the report): many more packages and
macros (natbib and biblatex citation commands, cleveref, hyperref, siunitx, algorithmic,
user macros that produce Markdown structure), citeproc-style bibliography rendering,
table column alignment and spans in Markdown, and the output of unknown environments.
Our reader expands user macros in text, but it does not load packages. Math is kept as
TeX in both.

Where we do more: figure* and subfigures are structured with captions, labels and image
candidates; labels and equation numbers are computed; BibTeX names, pages, dates, DOIs and
URLs are parsed into typed values; crossref cycles are handled.

Timing caveats. pandoc times are process wall time, including Haskell start-up. Our CLI
times include our process start-up too. The in-process figure measures parse and render
with no start-up. These are small files on one core, warm, with 20 runs and the median
reported. They do not show behaviour on large documents.
