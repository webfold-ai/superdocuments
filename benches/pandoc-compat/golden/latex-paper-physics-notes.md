# Maxwell's equations

In vacuum, with $\mathbf{E}$ the electric and $\mathbf{B}$ the magnetic field:

$$\begin{aligned}
\nabla\cdot\mathbf{E} &= \frac{\rho}{\varepsilon_0}, \\
\nabla\times\mathbf{B} &= \mu_0\mathbf{J} + \mu_0\varepsilon_0\frac{\partial\mathbf{E}}{\partial t}.
\end{aligned}$$

Equation [(1)](#eq:gauss) is Gauss's law. A charge of 1.6 × 10⁻¹⁹ C produces at 1 m a field of about 1.4 × 10⁻⁹ V/m.
